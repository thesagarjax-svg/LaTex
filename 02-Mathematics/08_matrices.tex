\section*{1. What is a matrix?}
A matrix is a rectangular arrangement of numbers or other mathematical entries in rows and columns.

\section*{2. A two-by-two matrix}
\textbf{LaTeX source code:}
\begin{verbatim}
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\]
\end{verbatim}
\textbf{Expected compiled output:}
\[
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2 \\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
\]

\textbf{Explanation:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \verb|bmatrix| creates a matrix surrounded by square brackets.
\item \verb|&| separates entries in the same row (columns).
\item \verb|\\| starts a new row.
\item The \verb|amsmath| package provides the \verb|bmatrix| environment.
\end{itemize}
